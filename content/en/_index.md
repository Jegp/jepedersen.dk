## Research focus

I develop **spatiotemporal computation frameworks for brain-inspired physical systems**,
bridging neuroscience and artificial intelligence. My work focuses on creating
practical tools and theoretical foundations for neuromorphic computing.

### Key projects

- [Neuromorphic Intermediate Representation (NIR)](https://neuroir.org) — A standard for bio-inspired computing
- [Spiking wavelets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02020) — Scale-covariant signal processing with spiking neurons
- [Faery](https://github.com/aestream/faery) — Event data processing
- [Geometric methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-63493-0) — For neuromorphic receptive fields
