A guest lecture for [27020 Bioengineering (Polytechnical Foundation)](https://kurser.dtu.dk/course/27020) at DTU on brain-inspired computing with spiking neural networks.

The lecture introduces BSc students to how the brain computes with spikes and why this matters for engineering, covering (1) how the brain computes, (2) spiking neural networks and neuromorphic hardware, (3) event cameras with a live demo, (4) [covariant spatio-temporal receptive fields](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-63493-0) for SNNs, (5) a hands-on Python exercise building a leaky integrate-and-fire neuron, and (6) the bigger picture: how we get from von Neumann to fully neuromorphic.
