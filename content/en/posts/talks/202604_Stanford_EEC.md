A guest lecture for 342 Energy Efficient Computing course at Stanford University, co-presented with [Steven Abreu](https://stevenabreu.com/) (University of Groningen).

The talk covers (1) the representation relation between physical systems and abstract models (Horsman, 2014), (2) computational classes and structure-preserving maps (Yanofsky, 2022), (3) source-independent linking and semantic retention for compiler correctness, (4) the 27 orders of magnitude energy gap, (5) von Neumann's mixed-signal insight, (6) axiomatizing the representation relation for neuromorphic systems, and (7) NIR as a cross-platform intermediate representation.
