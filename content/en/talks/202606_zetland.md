I was interviewed by Line Sand for the Danish magazine [Zetland](https://www.zetland.dk/historie/sRBm2bKm-moGPZYpN-162a3)
about neuromorphic computing and why the brain's energy efficiency has drawn so
much attention and investment.

The article — *"Din hjerne bruger latterligt lidt strøm. Derfor kaster Silicon
Valley milliarder efter den"* (in Danish) — covers what neuromorphic computing
is, the mix of analog and digital computation that the brain performs, why such
systems need not imitate the brain specifically, and the point that many useful
problems do not require superhuman intelligence in the first place.

An audio version is available for Zetland subscribers.
