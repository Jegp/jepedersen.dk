# The obstacle: the representational gap

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-8 items-center">
<div>

Our nomenclature was built for **digital, clocked, von Neumann** machines:

<v-clicks>

- Boolean logic, RAM model, dense matmul
- Computations **blind to the substrate's physics**
  - **Computes all possible paths**, ignore sparsity, pay translation costs
- Causes **inefficiency** and **slow adoption** of new substrates

</v-clicks>

</div>
<div>

<img src="/_assets/representation_mismatch.svg" class="h-40 mx-auto"/>
<p class="text-xs text-gray-400 text-center">Adapted from Horsman et al., 2014</p>


</div>
</div>

---

# What closes the gap

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-8 mt-6">
<div class="p-5 bg-green-50 rounded-lg">

## 1. Align with physics

<v-clicks>

- Let the **substrate** do the computation
- Physical primitives, not logic gates
- The ODE **is** the device

</v-clicks>

</div>
<div class="p-5 bg-blue-50 rounded-lg">

## 2. Exploit sparsity

<v-clicks>

- Only act when the **signal changes**
- Only compute the **relevant** paths

</v-clicks>

</div>
</div>

<v-click>

<div class="mt-6 p-3 bg-orange-50 rounded-lg text-center text-sm">

###  The challenge: find a representation that works for algorithms *and* hardware

</div>

</v-click>

# Neuromorphic Intermediate Representation

## - Train anywhere, deploy to physics

<div class="grid grid-cols-[3fr_2fr] gap-6 items-center">
<div>

A first step at closing the gap: a representation whose **primitives are the physics**.

<v-clicks>

- Primitives defined as **continuous-time ODEs**, not discrete ops
  - Analog dynamics + discrete spikes (von Neumann's *two* signals)
- Train anywhere &rarr; export to NIR &rarr; deploy
- **~12 simulators + 6 hardware platforms**, incl. Loihi, SpiNNaker, Innatera, SynSense Speck & Xylo, FPGA, with more to come

</v-clicks>

<br/>

<v-click>

> **First step towards consistency and reproducibility** for neuromorphics

</v-click>

</div>

# An axiomatic and physically faithful intermediate representation


<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-8 items-center">
<div>

What it would take to be a *calculus*:

<v-clicks>

- **composition** &mdash; how primitives combine
- **equivalence** &mdash; when two graphs perform the same computation

- **cost semantics** &mdash; so the gains are *derivable*
